WHY WE EXIST

Most sensing hardware is built for the environment it was tested in — not the one it ends up in. When conditions get harder, it degrades, drifts, or simply fails.

Tegmaro takes on the sensing problems that call for a different approach: novel transduction, distributed architectures, and compute pushed to the edge. We work on a small number of hard problems at a time, and stay with them past the prototype.
